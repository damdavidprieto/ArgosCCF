# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
What follows is a summary; the linked text is the one that applies.

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews)
and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, using the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/damdavidprieto). Every report will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that do not follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose
behavior is inappropriate, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
